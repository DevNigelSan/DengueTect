Protect your household

Dengue spreads through the Aedes mosquito, which breeds in clean, standing water. These four habits make the biggest difference.

Search & destroy

Empty, scrub, or cover anything that collects water: pails, old tires, flower vases, bottle caps, and roof gutters.

Protect yourself

Wear long sleeves and pants, use repellent, and sleep under a mosquito net or with screened windows.

Seek early check-up

If someone has a fever for 2 days or more, visit your barangay health center right away. Early care saves lives.

Support community action

Join clean-up drives and allow fogging or spraying when health workers announce it for your area.

Go to a hospital right away if someone with dengue has:

  • Severe stomach pain
  • Vomiting that won't stop
  • Bleeding from the nose or gums
  • Blood in vomit or stool
  • Unusual sleepiness or restlessness
  • Fast or difficult breathing

These can be signs of severe dengue, which needs emergency care, even after the fever goes down.
